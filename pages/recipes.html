Salad
Ingredients
  • To blanch: Kale, Broccoli, Baby corn, Leeks
  • Raw: Onion, Tomato, Lettuce, Cucumber, Sprouts
  • Boiled potato
For Dressing:
  • Lemon juice, Oregano
  • Salt, Black pepper
  • Olive oil
  • grated garlic (optional)
  • ginger paste (optional)
Instructions
  1. Choose an assortment of available vegetables
  2. Blanch required vegetables with salt
  3. Mix everything together
Ghee Rice with Gravy
  1. Soak bhasmati rice
  2. Fry cashews, raisins and onion - keep aside
  3. Fry bay leaf, star anise, cloves, cinnamon, elaichi, nutmeg, green chilli
  4. Add rice - fry for 2 more minutes
  5. Add salt, water and cook
For Gravy:
  1. Dry roast - 1 cup grated coconut, ginger, garlic, gasgase, onion, cashew, cinnamon, cloves, coriander seeds, sounf
  2. Grind with water
  3. Cook vegetables
  4. Add paste, salt, garam masala, ghee
Palak Paneer
  1. Boil palak & cashews in cooker for 1 whistle (add sugar here?)
  2. Fry jeera, garlic and onion - in that order, with wait inbetween
  3. Grind the boiled palak and cashews with corriander leaves
  4. Add the puree to the fried mixture and cook
  5. Add paneer cubes, salt, garam masala, little sugar
Cake
Ingredients
  • Sugar - 1.5 cups
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Wheat flour - 2.25 cups
  • Melted butter - 0.5 cup
  • Winner powder - 0.75 cup
  • Baking Soda - 1.5 tsp
  • Baking Powder - 0.5 tsp
Instructions
  1. Boil milk with sugar
  2. Preheat the oven
  3. Whisk everything together
  4. Bake at 200°C for 1 hour in OTG
  5. Let it cool before serving
Dry Fruits Laddoo
Ingredients for 80 laddoos
  • Almonds, Pista, Walnuts - 200g each
  • Cashew, Raisins - 150g each
  • Pumpkin, Flax, Sunflower seeds - 70g each
  • Soft Dates - 450g
  • Ghee
Instructions
  1. Dry roast and grind everything separately
  2. Ghee roast for dates and raisins
  3. Mix everything well and shape into laddoos
