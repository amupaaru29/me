Girls are still behind the Screens

For a month I’ve been touring
Around the remote villages developing
Just to know what they think
And what the girls there want to think

They told they want them,
The girls, to stitch all their clothes per hem
But the girls said they want to study
Even when the season is rainy

The elders have their own traditions
Giving the girls too many conditions
Oh, the girls were just too helpless
For the money they had was too less

In India, the population of boys
Has dominated the girls’ as toys
Though the government has passed laws
For a girl to live with no flaws

The girls are still behind the screens
Why don’t we just open those screens
Let the girls come out of their cage
And dance and win out on the stage!

Education is part and parcel of life
Like boys, girls too lead the same human life
Then why not just let them study
Bringing them out of old beliefs' custody?

Among the Stinking Wolves

I stood there for a while
Among the dangerous vile
For I did not know how to do
Whatever I had to do

I was stuck in such a situation
In, of course, the God’s creation
There I was all alone
But for the hungry tone

I’d been surrounded by those
Who made me hold up my nose
I couldn’t decide which was worse
That smell or the death curse

I’d been packed up with them
The deadly group of ‘em
Oh, how much I wished
To escape from the wolves nourished

Why not Monarchy?

When the leaders are so irresponsible
Themselves chanting 'Impossible'
Isn’t it perhaps thinkable, why not monarchy?

When drainages and garbages stink
And our leaders have no brain to think
I often look up and blink, why not Monarchy?

When no electricity comes through our sockets
But money overflows in their pockets
I'm in no mood to fly rockets
Oh, why not Monarchy?

When they've turned out to be so corrupt
Carelessly leaving the farmers bankrupt
I hear my own backbone crack up
Just tell me, why not Monarchy?

When the good stand against them rebelling
Their money and manpower mess up everything
Thus, I end up questioning, why not Monarchy?

Under the Influence

May be I look like I'm in tension
For I've lost all my attention
Among my peers, among my dears
Cz all I'd now shed is a few tears

Oh, if only I could rewind In reality, not just in my mind
Wanna go just a year back in time Cz that's when my life lost its chime

After all my reputed childhood years
It was never among any of my worst fears
To get into the world of thugs
Teens they still were, but traded drugs

It was a wrong place, a wrong day
Wrong move, wrong talk, wrong in every way
Nevertheless, I spent a year of glory
Inhaling or injecting it, I was in a frenzy

But who said this bliss was for eternity?
Now a year later, I'm not even worth charity
In vain I wonder if I could just quit
The euphoric trick of it, I'm sick of it

Ah, an albatross around my neck I bear I've become a brainless zombie, life's unfair
No 2nd chance, no going to France
All my silly dreams, lost in trance

Here my life ends, in just teenage
And here I lie dying..
Restless, soulless and at last, lifeless

Virus

Beware if you bear the virus they say
Beware if you don't wear the mask they say
Afraid of Swine Flu always they are
They say the flus are dangerous as they are

Nobody knows how silly it is
The common H1N1 virus it is
The virus doesn't attack you back
If your immunity is good, it's taken aback

However, those who already have diseases
must be careful before, into them the virus eases

She was Angry - A Catfight

Shruthi took her pencil box
And kept it on the window box
It was just a prank, but she was angry!

Namita once played with her ball
When she had been to the ball
The moment she knew, she was angry!

Hannah picked up her gown
which had actually fallen down
Accusing Hannah of theft, she was angry!

All girls through furious at her
Decided to casually talk to her
But when they tried, she was angry!

The class girls were now fed up
their friendship with her, they gave up
Being left alone in class now.. she was, again, angry!

Mother

A mother is a parent, a clarinet

She can be your land or a helping hand
She is always patient when we are patients

She could be a patrol sent to us by the Overlord
But she'll be the advisor when we are the leaders

She's already a caring file when we're still puerile
We're lucky to have a mother, to us she's never a bother

Many times we perturb her, but never does she pester us
She's either immortal or always transcendental
